## 👨‍💻 About me

I'm a **DevOps Engineer** who can't help but automate everything in sight. My relationship with IT started around the age of 10 — building basic websites, fixing hardware, reinstalling Windows for the whole neighbourhood, messing with routers and home servers… you know the drill.

That curiosity never went away. These days I design and maintain cloud-native infrastructure — Kubernetes clusters, GitOps pipelines, IaC — and I still spend my personal time tinkering with side projects. Not because I have to, but because **IT is genuinely my biggest passion**, not just a career.

I'm also keeping a close eye (and hands on keyboard) on the **AI space**: LLMs, AI-powered tooling, and how all of this is reshaping the way we work in infrastructure and platform engineering. If it's new and interesting, I want to understand it.

- 🚀 &nbsp;Working as a **DevOps Engineer**, happy where I am — always open to learning something new
- ☁️ &nbsp;Main playground: **Google Cloud Platform** + **Kubernetes**
- 🤖 &nbsp;Daily AI tools: **GitHub Copilot** and **Claude** — and actively exploring LLMs applied to DevOps workflows
- 🔐 &nbsp;Security is non-negotiable: WAF, zero trust, hardened configs — I want everything locked down by default
- 🧪 &nbsp;Personal projects: maybe not production-grade, but proof that my terminal is never idle
- 🔁 &nbsp;Philosophy: **automate early, iterate fast, monitor everything — and secure it all**
